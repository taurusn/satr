Linen's mark

Built like Loom's and Ferry's: the same woven square, the same 18-unit threads with round caps on a 24-unit grid, the same icon construction. The woven square is a drop cap, and long threads are the lines of a page: two beside it, one running under it. Linen is woven cloth with a line inside its name; in Arabic it is سطر, a line.
