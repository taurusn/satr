Reading in Linen

Linen opens a note as a page. The column holds the line you are reading, and nothing else on the screen asks for your eye. Wiki links read as names, like Ferry and Product build method, with the path shown on hover.

Why a reader

An editor shows the source. A reader shows the page. Most of the time a note is read, not written, and the reading should feel like paper: steady type, a calm measure, a page that keeps its place while an agent rewrites the file.

Note

Callouts are drawn in ink. Rust is kept for things that failed.

What it keeps

  • The line you were reading, across saves and restarts.
  • Your text size, per file.
  • What changed since you last looked.

Ferry: product requirements

6.4 Keyboard

Follow focus opens the phone's keyboard when a text field gets focus on the Mac, unless the person at the Mac moved focus in the last three seconds.

Typing safety: a deletion only applies inside the current typing epoch, so autocorrect never deletes across a click.

The key row carries Esc, Tab, the arrows and sticky modifiers. Sticky modifiers never apply to typed text.

6.5 Screen

The stream keeps at most two frames in flight and drops before encoding when the phone falls behind; keyframes on start, on request and when the region jumps.

Zoom follows the pointer and captures the region at full resolution.

القراءة في سطر

يفتح سطر الملاحظة صفحةً. العمود يحمل السطر الذي تقرؤه، ولا شيء آخر على الشاشة يطلب انتباهك. الروابط بين الملاحظات تُقرأ أسماءً، مثل فيري.

لماذا قارئ

المحرر يُظهر المصدر، والقارئ يُظهر الصفحة. أغلب الوقت تُقرأ الملاحظة ولا تُكتب، والقراءة يجب أن تشبه الورق: حرف ثابت، وعرض هادئ، وصفحة تحفظ مكانك حين يعيد وكيل كتابة الملف.

Mixed lines keep their own direction: this English sentence stays left to right inside an Arabic note.

ما يحفظه

  • السطر الذي كنت تقرؤه، بعد الحفظ وبعد إعادة التشغيل.
  • حجم الخط لكل ملف.
