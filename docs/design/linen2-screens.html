Linen 2

Boards, sealed pages, notes with blocks and export on the Mac; Linen on the iPhone with its own link to the Mac. Boards are drawn at reference scale (170 boxes in ten sections) by BRD-8's rules: no text under 9 px at rest, state as one of six ink shapes. Loom's tokens only, Thmanyah named and never embedded, sample content only. Built by docs/design/build_screens2.py on top of the v1 sketch.

A board at overview

The reference-scale board (170 boxes: 76 works, 52 partial, 10 broken, 14 missing, 18 planned) at Fit on a 13-inch window, 20 percent: a box is 48 px wide, so no title fits at 9 px and each box shows its state as its shape, while section names keep 15 px. The rail starts at the legend, and its rows work like find: Broken is selected, its boxes are ringed, ‹ › steps through them, and the current one shows its title and word in a small solid label.
