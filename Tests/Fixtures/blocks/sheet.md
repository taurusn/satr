# A sheet

A sheet is a wide page with a side column. Side blocks sit beside the prose that comes after them, once the window has room for both; on a narrow window they fall into the column in order.

```side
**Owner:** the requests team.

**Next review:** 21 October.

See [[Linked Note]].
```

The main column carries the argument. It runs at its measure on the left of the page, and the side column holds the facts a reader looks up while reading.
